## About Me

I'm Kris, a CAD designer and AI specialist with more than six years of
professional drafting and design experience. My background spans shipbuilding,
architectural, mechanical, construction, and 3D modeling work.

I'm exploring practical ways to combine that experience with artificial
intelligence—from improving design workflows to developing prototypes,
automations, websites, and applications.

## DesignForge AI

DesignForge AI explores how designers, engineers, CAD professionals, makers,
and creators can use artificial intelligence in real-world projects.

The focus is on learning through practical experimentation: understanding the
tools, testing ideas, solving problems, and sharing what works along with
the limitations.

This GitHub account provides a home for project files, code, documentation,
and resources that support that work.
