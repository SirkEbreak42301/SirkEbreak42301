## About Me

I'm Sirk, a CAD designer with more than six years of professional drafting
and design experience. My background spans shipbuilding, architectural,
mechanical, construction, and 3D modeling work.

I'm exploring practical ways to combine that experience with artificial
intelligence—from CAD and physical products to software, productivity
applications, websites, utilities, automation, and other useful projects.
I make the decisions, integrate the work, and evaluate the results.

## DesignForge AI

The mission is to use AI to help turn ideas and real-world problems into
useful things, then test whether those solutions actually work.

FORGE is my AI design, development, and problem-solving partner: a fictional
AI character representing the AI side of our collaboration. Depending on
the project, the actual tools may include ChatGPT, Codex, or other AI
technologies. FORGE can help analyze requirements, propose designs, write
or review code, develop workflows, troubleshoot, and suggest revisions.
AI can be wrong; I evaluate its suggestions and test the resulting solution.

**Sirk brings the problem. FORGE helps develop the solution. Together we
build it. Reality decides if it works.**

This GitHub account provides a home for project files, code, documentation,
and resources that support that work. Expect successes, limitations,
mistakes, and revisions—not just finished results.

### Project 001 — DesignForge Desktop Organizer

Our first physical Design & Engineering / Build With AI project uses an
existing STL as a reference for a planned Fusion 360 redesign. The target
envelope is 9 inches wide × 7 inches deep × 5 inches maximum height, with
two larger rear compartments, two smaller front compartments, and a
center phone/wallet area.

The design is intended for additive manufacturing. Printing, prototyping,
and physical testing will follow once manufacturing access is available.
Project files, development results, and the corresponding DesignForge AI
video series will be linked as the project progresses.
